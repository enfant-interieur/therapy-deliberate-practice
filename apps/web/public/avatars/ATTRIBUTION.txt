Therapy Studio — fictional patient avatar

Portrait: an original AI-generated fictional adult character prepared for Albert
Buchard's schema therapy Avatar studio. No patient image is used.

Model: portrait-fitted MPFB rig derived from the CC0 demonstration asset in
TalkingHead. Hair: MakeHuman long01, CC0 1.0. Clothing and seams were generated
locally for the character. The face color is fitted from the generated portrait.
- https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead#the-indexhtml-test-app
- https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The practice version retains the original triangle count and the jaw/blink morphs.
Textures are resized and compressed to WebP; geometry uses Meshopt compression.
